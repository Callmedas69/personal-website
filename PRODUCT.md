# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: Web3/crypto builders, on-chain degens, crypto-native devs. They arrive from Twitter/Farcaster, already aware of 0xdas. What they want: evidence the work is real. What they walk away with: "this person ships, thinks clearly, and is worth following."

Secondary: Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Harry as a candidate. They want to see signal through noise — not a polished portfolio, but undeniable proof of craft.

Tertiary: Social followers using the cognitive log as a window into Harry's build process. The log is the depth layer; the homepage is the hook.

## Product Purpose

A live cognitive operating system, not a portfolio. Expresses the 0xNull persona — Harry "0xdas" Herryanto, solo builder, Base-native, cognitively authentic. The site doesn't pitch; it documents. The AI terminal, mood system, on-chain integrations, and cognitive log are the product. Success: visitors feel they've observed a real mind at work, not read a resume.

## Brand Personality

Cognitive. Solo. Shipping.

Voice: lowercase, comment syntax (//), dry builder-brained, void energy. Always mid-thought. No hype, no self-congratulation, honest about failures. "void energy, always mid-thought." is the register, not just a footer line.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing page: gradient CTAs, metric callouts, "empower your workflow" copy, Webflow template structure.
- Developer portfolio cliché: "Hi, I'm Harry" hero, skills grid, project cards, contact form. The bootcamp portfolio format.
- NFT bro flexing: floor prices, Discord links, loud on-chain bragging. Crypto maximalism as identity.
- AI startup slop: cream background, rounded-everything, gradient text, next-gen buzzwords. The 2024–2026 Y Combinator aesthetic.
- Reference: Stripe Docs / Stripe Dev tools — dark terminal precision, Sohne typography, information-dense without noise. Already the baseline; maintain it.

## Design Principles

1. **Terminal as identity** — the interface is the persona. Build UI like a dev tool: structured, precise, no decorative chrome. Components should feel authored, not templated.
2. **Show the process, not the pitch** — cognitive log and mood states over skills lists and project cards. The log of failure is as valuable as the deploy.
3. **Void energy as direction** — always mid-thought. The site never "closes" or resolves into a sales funnel. It logs. Copy ends in continuation, not completion.
4. **On-chain proof over self-claim** — wallet connection and block updates are identity elements, not features. Being live on Base is a personality trait.
5. **Cognitive authenticity** — ADHD-native: fast to resume, nothing buried, honest labels. "// it broke" is a valid and visible state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum on body text: ≥4.5:1 contrast against background for all reading-weight text. Small decorative mono text at low opacity (e.g. `text-[9px] text-text-slate/40`) is intentional atmosphere and exempt. Reduced motion: all animations must have a `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` alternative. No formal WCAG level beyond AA body text.
